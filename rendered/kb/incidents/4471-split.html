#4471 was three problems, not one

owner lorimccurry open reviewed 2026-10-30 · 1 days ago

#4471 merges 412 reports under one story: the parser reads dates month first, day-first tenants get the day and month flipped. causes.ts already knows that story has two different bugs inside it — parser and hint. It does not know about a third one.

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone, never the body. `causeOf()` only ever asks a report's tenant and channel, never its body either. Nothing in the pipeline ever reads what a report actually says — so nothing in the pipeline could have found this on its own.

parser and hint are the two bugs the desk already modeled, counted exactly as the desk’s own export counts them — this page changes no counting logic, only adds a third row. timezone is not a day/month mix-up at all: it’s 5 reports where the invite’s calendar day depends on which timezone opened it, or showed up a day off with no month confusion in sight. desk-notes.json already named these 5 ids under oneDayMove — the desk noticed, and still filed every one of them under parser too, footnoted, because causeOf() has no third bucket to put them in. So parser’s 379 and timezone’s 5 overlap: same 412 reports, read a second way, not a different split of them.

Why these 5 aren’t parser or hint

A parser/hint report reads as a day↔month swap: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July” is 7/4 read as 4/7. None of these do that:

  • R-41202 — “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” Same month, one day off. Not a swap.
  • R-41209 — “Every booking in June is one day off. Only June.” Month-specific, not a day/month confusion.
  • R-41236 — “The invite arrived before I made the booking.” No date-order bug produces this at all.
  • R-41344 — “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” Two recipients, two timezones, two calendar days for one instant.
  • R-41620 — “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday.” One day off, one month, no swap.

Scanning all 412 bodies for the swap pattern (D Month / D Month, values exchanged) matches 403 of them. These 5 are the only ones that don’t, and they’re exactly the 5 desk-notes.json already flagged — independent confirmation, not a guess.
